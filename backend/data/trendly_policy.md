## 2. Returns

**2.1 Return window.** Items may be returned within **30 calendar days of the delivery date**. The window is counted from delivery, not from the order date. Requests made after 30 days are not eligible under any circumstance.

**2.2 Condition.** Items must be unworn, unwashed, and returned with original tags attached and original packaging where provided.

**2.3 Non-returnable categories.** The following cannot be returned or exchanged for hygiene and safety reasons:
- Innerwear and socks
- Jewellery
- Beauty and fragrance products
- Face masks
- Gift cards

**2.4 Final sale items.** Items marked **final sale** are eligible for **size exchange only** - no refunds and no store credit. The 30-day window still applies.

**2.5 Footwear.** Footwear is returnable but must be returned in its original shoe box. Returns without the box incur a ₹300 deduction.

**2.6 Cancelled orders.** Once an order is cancelled, no return can be raised against it. Cancellation refunds follow the timelines in section 3.

---

## 3. Refunds

**3.1 Timelines.** After a returned item is received and passes inspection at the warehouse (2-3 business days), refunds are issued as follows:

| Original payment method | Refund destination | Time after inspection |
|---|---|---|
| Credit / debit card | Original card | 5-7 business days |
| UPI | Original UPI ID | 3-5 business days |
| Cash on delivery | Bank transfer or store credit | 7-10 business days |
| Store credit | Store credit | Immediate |

**3.2 Shipping fees.** The original ₹99 shipping fee is refunded only if the return is due to a Trendly error (wrong item, damaged item, defective item). It is not refunded for change-of-mind returns.

**3.3 Cash-on-delivery refunds.** These require bank account details, which are collected by a human agent over a secure link. The assistant must never collect bank details in chat.

**3.4 Partial refunds.** If only some items in an order are returned, only those items are refunded. Free-shipping eligibility is not recalculated.

---

## 4. Exchanges

**4.1 Scope.** Trendly offers **size exchanges only** - not colour or style exchanges. To change colour or style, the customer returns the item and places a new order.

**4.2 Window.** Same 30-day window as returns.

**4.3 Availability.** If the requested size is unavailable, the exchange is automatically converted to a refund under section 3.

**4.4 Limit.** One exchange per item. A second exchange request on the same item requires human approval.

---

## 5. Return pickup

**5.1 Pickup.** Free reverse pickup is available on all serviceable pincodes. The customer schedules a pickup window when raising the return; the carrier attempts pickup up to 2 times.

**5.2 Non-serviceable pincodes.** The customer self-ships to the Trendly warehouse and is reimbursed up to ₹150 in courier costs against a receipt.

**5.3 Failed pickups.** After 2 failed pickup attempts, the return is closed and must be re-raised - provided the original 30-day window has not expired.

---

## 6. Damaged or wrong items

**6.1 Reporting window.** Damaged, defective, or incorrect items must be reported within **48 hours of delivery**, with photographs.

**6.2 Resolution.** Trendly ships a replacement at no cost, or issues a full refund including shipping, at the customer's choice. Non-returnable categories (section 2.3) are covered by this clause when the item arrives damaged or incorrect.
